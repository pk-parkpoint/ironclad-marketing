## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs You Need Fixture Service
- **Intro:** Fixtures are finish details, but the plumbing behind them still has to be tight, level, sealed, and tested.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **You're installing new fixtures** — New faucets, sinks, toilets, tubs, and shower parts need compatible rough-in and clean connections.
  2. **A fixture leaks or drips** — Drips, base leaks, and supply leaks waste water and damage cabinets, flooring, and walls.
  3. **Flow or pressure feels wrong** — Low flow, uneven temperature, or weak fixture performance often comes from valves, cartridges, or buildup.
  4. **A remodel needs plumbing support** — Kitchen, bath, laundry, and utility upgrades need fixture selection and installation planned around existing plumbing.
