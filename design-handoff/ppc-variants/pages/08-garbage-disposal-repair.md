## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs Your Disposal Needs Service
- **Intro:** A disposal usually warns you before it quits. Listen and look for these.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **It hums but won't spin** — A humming disposal that will not turn is jammed or has a seized motor.
  2. **It leaks under the sink** — Water under the unit means a failed seal, flange, or cracked housing.
  3. **It clogs or drains slowly** — Frequent clogs point to worn blades or a drain issue below the unit.
  4. **Persistent odor or noise** — Grinding noise or a smell that won't clear signals trouble inside.

- **Intro:** We fix what can be fixed and replace what can't, cleanly, in one visit.

  2. **Leak repair** — Seals, flanges, and connections replaced to stop under-sink leaks.

  5. **New disposal installation** — Adding a disposal? We install and wire it to code.
