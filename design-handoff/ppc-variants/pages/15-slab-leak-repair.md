## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs of a Slab Leak
- **Intro:** Leaks under the foundation are sneaky. These are the tells to watch for.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **Warm spots on the floor** — A warm patch on the floor often marks a hot-water slab leak.
  2. **The sound of running water** — Water heard under the floor with everything off is a slab-leak sign.
  3. **Cracks in floors or walls** — Shifting from moisture under the slab can crack tile and drywall.
  4. **High bills and low pressure** — Water lost under the slab shows up as higher bills and weaker flow.

- **Intro:** We find it precisely and choose the repair that disturbs your home the least.
- **Cards 1–6** (photos: see `PHOTO-LIST.md` → 15. Slab leak repair):
  1. **Slab leak location** — Electronic and acoustic tools pinpoint the leak under the slab.
  2. **Spot repair** — Access and repair at the exact leak, minimizing floor removal.
  3. **Pipe rerouting** — Rerouting a failed line above the slab to avoid major demolition.
  4. **Repipe when needed** — When one slab leak signals more, we plan a lasting fix.
  5. **Hot & cold line repair** — Leaks on either supply line located and repaired.
  6. **Restoration coordination** — We document the work to support your repairs and claim.
