## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs You Have a Water Leak
- **Intro:** Leaks hide. These clues point to one before it does real damage to your home.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **Your water bill keeps climbing** — A steadily rising bill with no change in use is a classic hidden-leak sign.
  2. **Damp spots, stains, or mold** — Discoloration or a musty smell means moisture is escaping where it shouldn't.
  3. **The meter moves with water off** — If the meter ticks when everything is off, water is leaking somewhere.
  4. **Low pressure or warm floors** — Weak pressure or a warm spot on the floor can signal a hidden or slab leak.
