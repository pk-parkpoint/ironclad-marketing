## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs Your Water Heater Needs Repair
- **Intro:** Don't wait for a cold shower or a flooded closet. These are the early warnings.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **No hot water, or not enough** — Water that will not heat, or runs out fast, points to a failing element or thermostat.
  2. **Rusty or discolored hot water** — Brown or metallic hot water often means corrosion inside the tank.
  3. **Popping or rumbling sounds** — Sediment on the tank bottom makes noise and cuts efficiency.
  4. **Water pooling around the tank** — Any water at the base needs attention before the tank fails completely.
