## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs You Need Plumbing Repairs
- **Intro:** Small plumbing problems get expensive when they sit. These are the everyday issues worth fixing before they damage cabinets, walls, floors, or fixtures.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **Faucets keep dripping** — A drip after the handle shuts off usually means worn internal parts, pressure issues, or corrosion that should be repaired before it worsens.
  2. **Toilets run or flush weakly** — Constant running wastes water, and a weak flush points to failing tank parts, a blockage, or a fixture that needs service.
  3. **Water pressure drops** — Low or uneven pressure can come from bad valves, buildup, leaks, or regulator issues that need a real diagnosis.
  4. **Stains, corrosion, or pooling appear** — Water marks, rusted fittings, and puddles under cabinets mean the repair should happen before hidden damage spreads.

## Section 3 — Services  `data-slot="services-title"`
- **Heading:** Our Plumbing Repair Services
- **Intro:** We diagnose the issue, explain the fix, and give you written pricing before work begins.
- **Cards 1–6** (photos: see `PHOTO-LIST.md` → 21. Plumbing repairs):
  1. **Leak and drip repair** — Faucets, supply lines, exposed pipe leaks, and under-sink problems repaired cleanly.
  2. **Toilet repair** — Running toilets, weak flushes, clogs, wax seals, and tank parts fixed or replaced.
  3. **Valve replacement** — Failed shutoffs, angle stops, hose bibs, and control valves replaced before they cause an emergency.
  4. **Pressure troubleshooting** — Low pressure, uneven flow, and suspected regulator problems diagnosed and corrected.
  5. **Fixture repair** — Faucets, sinks, tubs, showers, and connected parts repaired when replacement is not needed.
  6. **General pipe and fitting repair** — Worn fittings, corrosion, and small pipe failures repaired with tested workmanship.
