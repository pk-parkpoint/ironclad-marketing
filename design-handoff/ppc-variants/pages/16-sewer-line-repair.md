## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs of a Sewer Line Problem
- **Intro:** Sewer trouble affects the whole house. These signs mean call now.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **Multiple drains backing up** — When several drains back up at once, the main sewer line is the cause.
  2. **Gurgling toilets** — Toilets that gurgle when other fixtures run signal a main-line blockage.
  3. **Sewage smell inside or out** — A sewage odor points to a break or blockage in the line.
  4. **Soggy patches in the yard** — Wet or extra-green patches over the line can mean a break below.

## Section 3 — Services  `data-slot="services-title"`
- **Heading:** Our Sewer Line Services
- **Intro:** We diagnose by camera first, then fix it with the right method for your line.
- **Cards 1–6** (photos: see `PHOTO-LIST.md` → 16. Sewer line repair / replacement):
  1. **Camera sewer inspection** — An HD camera shows the exact problem and its location.
  2. **Sewer line clearing** — Blockages and roots cleared to restore flow.
  3. **Spot & section repair** — Damaged sections repaired without replacing the whole line.
  4. **Sewer line replacement** — Collapsed or failing lines replaced to last.
  5. **Root intrusion removal** — Roots cut out and the line protected against regrowth.
  6. **Trenchless options** — Less-dig repair and replacement where the line allows.
