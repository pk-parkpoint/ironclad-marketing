## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** When to Call an Emergency Plumber
- **Intro:** If any of these are happening, don't wait until morning. Call now and we will get a plumber moving.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **Water won't stop** — A burst pipe or overflowing fixture flooding your home needs the water shut off and stopped fast.
  2. **No water at all** — Sudden loss of water to the whole house points to a main break or a failed valve.
  3. **Sewage backing up** — Waste coming up through drains or toilets is a health hazard that can't wait.
  4. **A gas smell** — The smell of gas means leave the home and call immediately; we respond 24/7.
