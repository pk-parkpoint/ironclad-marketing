## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs Your Faucet Needs Attention
- **Intro:** A small faucet problem wastes water and usually means a quick, affordable fix.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **A drip that won't stop** — A constant drip wastes gallons and usually means a worn cartridge or washer.
  2. **Low or uneven pressure** — Weak flow points to a clogged aerator or a supply issue.
  3. **Handles are loose or stiff** — Handles that wobble or stick signal worn internal parts.
  4. **Rust, buildup, or base leaks** — Corrosion or pooling at the base means it's time to repair or replace.
