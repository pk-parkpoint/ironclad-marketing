## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs Your Water Heater Needs Service
- **Intro:** Hot water problems usually start with clear warning signs. Catch them early and you may avoid a full failure, leak, or emergency replacement.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **No hot water or swinging temperatures** — Cold showers, lukewarm water, or temperature swings point to controls, elements, gas, or capacity issues.
  2. **Hot water runs out fast** — A tank that cannot keep up may be undersized, full of sediment, or near the end of its service life.
  3. **Rusty water or tank noise** — Rust-colored water, popping, rumbling, or banging often points to corrosion or sediment buildup.
  4. **Water appears near the heater** — Pooling around the base, pan, or relief line should be checked quickly before damage spreads.
