## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs Your Toilet Needs a Plumber
- **Intro:** Some toilet trouble is more than a plunger job. Watch for these before a small issue floods the bathroom.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **It won't flush or clears slowly** — A weak or failed flush points to a clog, a fill problem, or a blocked vent.
  2. **Water runs constantly** — A toilet that keeps running wastes gallons a day and usually means a worn flapper or fill valve.
  3. **It rocks or leaks at the base** — Water around the base signals a failed wax seal; left alone it damages the subfloor.
  4. **It clogs again and again** — Repeat clogs can mean a partial blockage downstream or a low-flow toilet that struggles.

## Section 3 — Services  `data-slot="services-title"`
- **Heading:** Our Toilet Repair Services
- **Intro:** From a running tank to a full replacement, we handle it in one visit when we can.
- **Cards 1–6** (photos: see `PHOTO-LIST.md` → 03. Clogged toilet / toilet repair):
  1. **Clogged toilet clearing** — Stubborn clogs cleared with an auger, cleanly and without damaging the bowl.
  2. **Running toilet repair** — Worn flappers, fill valves, and flush valves replaced so the tank seals and stops running.
  3. **Leak & wax seal repair** — Base leaks fixed with a new seal and bolts before they reach the subfloor.
  4. **Toilet installation** — New toilets set level and sealed right, with the old one hauled away.
  5. **Fill & flush valve replacement** — Internal parts swapped for a strong, quiet, reliable flush.
  6. **Overflow cleanup** — We fix the cause and clean up the overflow so the bathroom is left dry.
