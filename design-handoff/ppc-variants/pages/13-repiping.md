## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs Your Home Needs Repiping
- **Intro:** Old pipe fails in patterns. These signs mean it's the system, not just one spot.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **Repeated leaks** — When one pipe after another leaks, the system, not the spot, is the problem.
  2. **Discolored or metallic water** — Rust-tinted water points to corroding galvanized or aging pipe.
  3. **Low pressure throughout** — Corrosion narrowing old pipe drops pressure across the whole house.
  4. **Old galvanized or polybutylene** — These materials are known to fail and are worth replacing proactively.

  6. **Drain & sewer repiping** — Failing drain lines replaced along with supply where needed.
