## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs You Have a Clogged Drain
- **Intro:** A clog rarely fixes itself, and the wrong tools can make it worse. These are the signs it's time to stop plunging and call a pro.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **Water backs up when you drain** — Water rising in the sink, tub, or another fixture when you run the drain means a clog is blocking the flow.
  2. **It drains slower every day** — A drain that empties a little slower each week is a clog steadily building toward a full backup.
  3. **Plunging only works for a while** — If the clog keeps returning after you plunge, it's lodged deeper than household tools can reach.
  4. **A bad smell from the drain** — Trapped food, grease, or waste behind a clog gives off an odor that won't rinse away.

## Section 3 — Services  `data-slot="services-title"`
- **Heading:** Our Clogged Drain Services
- **Intro:** Whatever is blocked, we find it and clear it at the source, then make sure it flows before we leave.
- **Cards 1–6** (photos: see `PHOTO-LIST.md` → 02. Clogged drain):
  1. **Kitchen sink clogs** — Grease and food buildup cleared from the drain and trap so water runs freely again.
  2. **Bathroom sink & shower clogs** — Hair and soap scum removed from slow bathroom drains, down to the branch line.
  3. **Toilet clogs** — Stubborn toilet clogs cleared with an auger, without cracking the bowl or making a mess.
  4. **Main line clogs** — A whole-house backup usually means the main line. We clear it at the cleanout.
  5. **Snaking & augering** — Professional-grade cables reach and break up clogs far past the fixture.
  6. **Hydro jetting** — For the toughest, greasiest clogs, high-pressure jetting scours the pipe wall clean.
