## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs Your Sump Pump Is Failing
- **Intro:** A sump pump only matters when it works. Test it before you need it, and watch for these.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **It runs constantly or cycles fast** — Nonstop running or rapid cycling points to a stuck switch or the wrong size pump.
  2. **Strange noises or vibration** — Grinding or rattling means a worn impeller or motor.
  3. **It won't turn on** — A pump that stays silent when the pit fills is the worst-case failure.
  4. **Visible rust or old age** — Rust, or a pump over 7 to 10 years old, is due for replacement.
