## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs of a Gas Line Problem
- **Intro:** Gas issues are safety issues. If you notice these, act right away.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **A rotten-egg or gas smell** — That smell is added to gas as a warning; leave and call immediately.
  2. **A hissing sound near a line** — Hissing at a pipe or appliance connection can mean escaping gas.
  3. **Dead plants near the line** — Vegetation dying in a patch over a buried line can signal a leak.
  4. **A higher gas bill** — A bill that rises with no change in use may point to a leak.
