## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs You Need Drain Cleaning
- **Intro:** Drains almost never fail without warning; they slow down, gurgle, and smell before they ever back up. Catching those warnings early is the difference between a quick clear and water on the floor. Here are the four that matter most.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **Water drains slowly** — A sink, tub, or shower that takes forever to empty is the earliest warning. Buildup is narrowing the pipe, and it only gets tighter from here.
  2. **The same clog keeps coming back** — You clear it, it works for a few days, then slows down again. A returning clog almost always means buildup deeper in the line than a plunger can reach.
  3. **Gurgling sounds or sewer smells** — Gurgling after the water runs, or an odor rising from the drain, points to trapped air or a partial blockage. The drain is telling you something is stuck.
  4. **More than one drain backs up at once** — When several fixtures act up together, the problem isn't the fixture; it's the main line. This one deserves a call today.

  2. **Shower, tub & floor drains** — Standing water and slow bathroom drains cleared out, including the floor drains that only act up when it rains.

  5. **Hydro jetting** — A high-pressure water jet scours grease, scale, and tree roots off the pipe walls, not just poking a hole through the clog.
  6. **Sewer camera inspection** — An HD camera down the line shows exactly what's going on, so you're not paying to guess at the problem.
