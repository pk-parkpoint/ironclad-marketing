## Section 2 — Signs  `data-slot="signs-title"`
- **Heading:** Signs of a Burst or Failing Pipe
- **Intro:** Catching a pipe problem early keeps a small repair from becoming a flood. Watch for these.
- **Rows 01–04:**
  1. **A sudden drop in water pressure** — Pressure that falls off a cliff can mean water is escaping through a break.
  2. **Water stains or damp spots** — Stains on walls, ceilings, or floors show water escaping behind them.
  3. **A spike in your water bill** — A bill that jumps for no reason often traces back to a hidden broken pipe.
  4. **Puddles or running-water sounds** — Water pooling, or the sound of running water with everything off, signals a break.
