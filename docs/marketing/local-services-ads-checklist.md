# Ironclad Local Services Ads Checklist

Last updated: 2026-07-14

This is an owner-assisted setup. Search campaigns are API-managed; Local Services Ads verification requires business documents, identity checks, and Google Business Profile access that cannot be completed from the repository.

## Business identity

- [ ] Sign in with the owner/manager account authorized for Ironclad's Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Use the exact public business name shown on the Business Profile and insurance documents.
- [ ] Set phone to `(512) 506-2470` and confirm a person answers 24/7 before enabling a 24/7 schedule.
- [ ] Enter `RMP #39871` exactly and upload the current license document if requested.
- [ ] Confirm the service category is Plumbing and select only services Ironclad actually performs.
- [ ] Use the same Greater Austin service area as the Search build; do not add distant counties to chase volume.

## Verification documents

- [ ] Link a public, verified Google Business Profile. The person completing LSA verification must be an owner or manager of that profile.
- [ ] Upload a current, signed Certificate of Insurance with policy number, adequate coverage, matching business name/DBA, and an address that covers the service area.
- [ ] Complete every business, owner, representative, and fieldworker identity/background check shown in the LSA dashboard.
- [ ] Add all fieldworkers who perform services at customer properties when Google requests a roster.
- [ ] Resolve any business-name mismatch with supporting DBA/registration documents instead of changing public branding ad hoc.
- [ ] Complete billing, budget, and any minimum-review requirement shown for the account.

Google says requirements vary by category and location and can include business registration, insurance, license, identity, and background checks. Its current guidance says screening commonly takes 3–4 weeks, so submit documents immediately. See [US screening requirements](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/12174778?hl=en), [screening process](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6226575?hl=en), and [document upload instructions](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/11455354?hl=en).

## Profile quality and lead controls

- [ ] Confirm the Business Profile and LSA profile both show `(512) 506-2470`, correct hours, correct license, and current service areas.
- [ ] Add real truck, team, and job photos without promotional text overlays.
- [ ] Enable phone leads. Enable messages only if someone can respond immediately.
- [ ] Route notifications to at least two accountable people and test every notification channel.
- [ ] Set the budget deliberately and document the weekly cap before going live.
- [ ] Record lead outcome, job type, revenue, and dispute reason in the operating system for every lead.
- [ ] Review calls and dispute objectively invalid leads within Google's allowed window.
- [ ] Ask completed customers for Google reviews without incentives or review gating.

## Go-live gate

- [ ] Verification dashboard shows all required checks complete or explicitly eligible for pre-badge serving.
- [ ] A test call reaches the answering team and caller ID/recording disclosures work as intended.
- [ ] The answering team can quote the Ironclad service area, book a job, and distinguish emergencies from non-service inquiries.
- [ ] The linked Business Profile is correct and has no duplicate Ironclad location competing with it.
- [ ] The owner approves the LSA budget and lead-response ownership.

After launch, review lead quality and missed-call rate every day for two weeks. LSA ranking and eligibility depend in part on responsiveness, profile quality, reviews, and continued compliance; Google can request repeat verification.
